Animation Swap

Back up the car file. Close the game before installing the output.

1. Open a car ZIP or carclips_<ID>.clipd.
2. Choose FRONT DOORS or REAR DOORS, then a donor car.
3. Apply donor to the selected animation. Front and rear can use different donors.
4. Save As writes a new file. A raw CLIPD must go back inside its car ZIP.

- Fix DB ride height / spacers removes animation overrides that can hide DB stance edits.
- Manage CLIPD channels adds or removes selected channels. Existing IDs are kept.
- Adjust start/end changes the selected channel's position and rotation.
- Reset discards pending edits.
- Non-door channels are experimental and may need a matching model or skeleton.
